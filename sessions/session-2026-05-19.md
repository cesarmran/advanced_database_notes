## What I understood
- A KPI is a key indicator that helps measure if a process, project, area, or business is achieving its goals.
- Not every metric is a KPI because a KPI must be directly related to an important objective.
- KPIs are useful because they make performance easier to evaluate with concrete data.
- I understood that KPIs can be used in different areas such as sales, marketing, operations, finance, customer service, and human resources.
- Some examples of KPIs are sales growth, customer satisfaction, conversion rate, profit margin, employee turnover, and delivery time.
- KPIs should be clear, measurable, and connected to a specific goal.
- A good KPI helps identify if something is working well or if changes are needed.
- KPIs are also important because they support decision-making instead of making decisions only based on opinion.
