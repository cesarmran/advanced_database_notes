## What I understood
- OLTP means Online Transaction Processing, and it is used for daily operations in a system.
- OLTP databases are focused on handling many small transactions quickly, such as sales, payments, registrations, orders, or updates.
- OLTP is usually used in systems where data changes constantly.
- OLAP means Online Analytical Processing, and it is used to analyze large amounts of data.
- OLAP databases are focused on reading and analyzing information instead of making many changes all the time.
- OLAP is useful for reports, dashboards, business intelligence, and decision-making.
- I understood that OLTP answers questions about what is happening right now in the business, while OLAP helps understand patterns, trends, and results over time.
- OLTP usually works with more detailed and current data, while OLAP works with historical and summarized data.
- A company can use both systems because OLTP helps operate the business and OLAP helps analyze the business.
- The main difference is that OLTP is more focused on transactions, while OLAP is more focused on analysis.
